# Reader changelog

Who reads it: people on a Mac who tile their windows or want to, often coming from yabai, AeroSpace,
skhd or sketchybar. They know what a workspace and a hotkey are, and they open the page to see what a
new version changes for them.

What goes in: what they will notice in their windows, the bar, the settings window and the menu bar,
and why it helps. Leave out refactoring, tests, CI and fixes nobody saw.

How: plain sentences in the second person where it fits, no marketing words, one thing per item. Name
a key of `kumiko.toml` only when the item is about it. A link goes to the landing page or to GitHub.

Each language is written in it, not translated: the Russian says «ты» and reads like a person wrote
it.
